What is Gemini AI?

Gemini AI is a beginner-safe market dashboard designed to help investors explore stock and crypto ideas with strong risk controls and easy-to-read signals. The tool focuses on simple allocation guidance, clear entry/exit levels, and a conservative view on trade setup quality.

How it works

The dashboard combines market data and AI-generated ranking logic to surface safer trade ideas. It is intended as a companion research tool rather than financial advice, helping users make better decisions with a clear, structured view of proposed entries, targets, and stops.

Why these pages?

These informational pages provide transparency around how the app is intended to be used, how to contact the developer, and the privacy/disclaimer terms that visitors should see when using the analyzer.
